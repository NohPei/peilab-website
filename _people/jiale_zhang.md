Jiale is a Ph.D. candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Michigan, co-advised by [Pei Zhang](../pei_zhang) and [Junyi Zhu](https://sixlab-eecs.com/). Jiale's research focuses on adaptive vibration sensing systems that combine physics-based modeling with data-driven inference. This work uses passive observation and active excitation to turn everyday structures into reliable sensing platforms, with validation through lab prototyping and real-world deployments.
